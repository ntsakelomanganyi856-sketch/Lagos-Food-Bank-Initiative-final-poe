About Lagos Food Bank Initiative

Our Mission

To provide nutritious food to vulnerable persons through sustainable food banking, partnerships, and community empowerment in Lagos.

Our Vision

A Lagos where no one goes to bed hungry, and where food waste is transformed into hope and dignity.

Why We Exist

Lagos generates over 13,000 tonnes of waste daily, 40% of it is food. Over 2 million people face food insecurity. We bridge that gap.

Founded in 2016

Started in Yaba with 3 volunteers, now 2,000+ volunteers serving 50,000 families monthly.
